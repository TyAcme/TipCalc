💡 Tipping Guide
Quick rules of thumb by service
Quick Rules of Thumb
  • 15–20% is the broad default for most personal and table-service work.
  • Flat dollar amounts are more common for hotels, valets, and simple bar drinks.
  • Counter/fast-casual and pure grab-and-go usually require little or no tip (despite tablet prompts).
  • Tips are customary in the U.S. for these roles because many workers earn a lower "tipped minimum wage."
